✦ Built for your circle

Social, without the noise.

There is no feed ranking deciding who you hear from, no suggested strangers, and no reach to chase. VipKorner does a small number of things — posting, stories, following, messaging — and then gets out of the way.

01 — Share moments

Photos and video, with room to say something.

Post a photo or a video with a caption and it goes to the people who follow you. Nothing else. You can edit a caption after the fact, delete a post outright, and see exactly who has responded to it.

Stories work the way you would expect: images or video, visible for 24 hours, then gone without you having to clean anything up.

  • Images and video in both posts and stories
  • Edit captions and delete your own posts at any time
  • A quick emoji reaction on a story, when its owner allows replies

02 — Find your people

Follow the people you already know.

Explore shows public profiles inside the community — no strangers pushed at you, no ranking, no "people you may know" assembled from a contact list you never handed over. You search, you look, you follow.

Your own follower and following lists are yours to read. Everyone else sees the counts and nothing more. If someone should not be in your korner, blocking them is one action and it holds across posts, stories and messages.

  • Public profiles by default, private account available in settings
  • Only you can open your follower and following lists
  • Follow, unfollow and block from any profile

03 — Talk privately

Conversations that stay between two people.

Direct messages are text only, deliberately. There is no forwarding, no group thread to get pulled into, and no media to leak out of a conversation you thought was closed.

Who can reach you follows the same rules as everything else: your follow and block settings decide it. Unread counts appear on every layout — desktop, compact and mobile — and refresh while the app is open, so you are never hunting for the conversation that moved.

  • Text-only, one to one, no groups
  • Governed by the same follow and block controls
  • Message someone straight from their profile

✦ Twenty-four hours

Some things are not meant to be permanent.

A story lasts a day and then removes itself. No archive to prune, no timeline to be embarrassed by later. If the person who posted it has story replies switched on, you can leave a single emoji — tap it again and it's gone.

✦ The door

Everyone here was invited by someone.

There is no open signup. Joining takes an active, unused invitation code, and every code can only be spent once — which is what keeps the community the size and shape it is.

01

A code that works once

Registration requires an active, unused invitation. When your account is confirmed, the code is claimed and cannot be used again by anyone else.

02

Adults only, checked properly

Your date of birth is verified on the server, not in the browser where it could be edited. Anyone under 18 is turned away — and the date itself is never stored.

03

Confirmed by email

Following the link in your confirmation email completes your profile, claims the invitation, signs you in and drops you straight into the app.

✦ On your home screen

It installs like an app, because it is one.

VipKorner is a progressive web app. Add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon, no store, no download, no separate build to keep updated. When you lose signal it tells you plainly instead of failing silently.
